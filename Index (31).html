02 / Our approach

Clinical alignment & policy

The CRC’s approach aligns with modern orthodontics and orthotropics. We oppose extraction of healthy teeth solely for cosmetic alignment. Members receive up to date, research backed information to help ensure unnecessary extraction does not occur and to prepare for informed consent discussions.

05 / What we stand for

Informed decisions come first.

  • ✓
    Informed consent. We help prepare questions and case studies for your orthodontist.
  • ✓
    No healthy tooth extractions for cosmetics. CRC advocates non extraction paths when medically appropriate.
  • ✓
    Education first. Members learn to read research, separate claims from evidence, and track progress honestly.
